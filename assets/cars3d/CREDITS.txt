with the game. Keep this file in the build, and keep these credits on the store page.

Only CC Attribution models were used. ShareAlike and NonCommercial models were deliberately
excluded -- ShareAlike would try to pull the game under its own terms, and NonCommercial
rules out commercial release outright.

Meshes are unmodified; they were re-rendered from a fixed camera angle, and one was recoloured
to a second in-game livery. In-game car names are Quantum Melanin Media's own -- no
affiliation with, or endorsement by, any vehicle manufacturer is claimed or implied.

Track centrelines for the real circuits are (c) OpenStreetMap contributors (ODbL), via
TUMFTM/racetrack-database.
